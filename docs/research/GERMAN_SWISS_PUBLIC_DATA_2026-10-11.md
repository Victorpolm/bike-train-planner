# German-speaking Swiss public transport: public bicycle and fare evidence

Reviewed: 11 October 2026. Research ledger, not a claim that every finding is implemented. Live bicycle-space inventory and ticket sales are outside the requested scope. Facts below are paraphrased from official public sources; no operator was contacted. Price figures are CHF. An undated web page's review date does not establish its future effective period.

## Findings that change the implementation plan

- The information is substantially public. Most examined networks publish regular fares, reduced-fare bicycle formulas, zone maps and detailed conditions. Production fare access is not a prerequisite for displaying a correctly scoped published tariff.
- The missing engineering is principally exact journey-to-tariff mapping, operator/line exceptions, profile applicability and version management. A production quotation service is useful for broad coverage and dynamic products; it is distinct from the publicly available standard tariff.
- “Reduced half fare” is a fare-table column, not always adult price divided by two. For example A-Welle one zone is 3.80 full / 2.90 reduced; TNW one zone is 4.20 / 2.90.
- An operator's membership of a fare network does not establish national bicycle-pass acceptance. OSTWIND warns of exceptions; Zugersee and Ägerisee shipping explicitly reject the national bicycle day pass and Velo-Pass.
- Public sources sometimes conflict. Preserve conflicts rather than deriving an apparently exact answer from whichever paragraph is easiest to parse.

## Structured data and automatic renewal

1. **ZVV tariff-zone geography:** the [official cantonal dataset](https://opendata.swiss/de/dataset/tarifzonen-des-offentlichen-verkehrs) advertises WFS/WMS. It is maintained for ZVV using VBZ stop data. This is a useful machine-readable zoning source, not a fare engine. The dataset resource response was not successfully opened in this review; its endpoint, schema, update cadence and reuse terms still need implementation verification.
2. **A-Welle full tariff:** [T651.20, 1 June 2026](https://www.a-welle.ch/fileadmin/seiteninhalt/dokumente/2026/T651.20_barrierefrei_20260601.pdf) includes operator/line membership, overlap/master-tariff rules, prices and stop-to-zone appendix 10.1. Extract into versioned records, validate exact stop IDs, retain source/version, and test through-zones and overlap cases. The public PDF is structured enough for extraction; no documented public production quote API was found here.
3. **Other networks:** public HTML/PDF tables and tariff version indexes can be fetched automatically to discover changes. OSTWIND's HTML numeric rows depend on client-side rendering; use official tariff download rather than assuming an undocumented shop endpoint is a supported public API.
4. **Future tariffs:** Passepartout already publishes its 13 December 2026 values. Acquire the next version before expiry, compare parsed values/rules, run effective-date transition cases, approve the new version, then activate by Swiss travel date.
5. **Evidence status:** store `sourceUrl`, `sourceVersion`, `retrievedAt`, `effectiveFrom`, optional publisher `effectiveThrough`, independent `reviewBy`, exact operator/mode/line scope and unresolved conflicts. A content hash is change detection, not proof that price or meaning changed.

No anonymous, documented production fare API covering all of the above networks was established in this bounded research. This is an absence of confirmed API evidence, not a claim that such data cannot be obtained or that standard local fares cannot be implemented.

## Recommended safe additions and outstanding work

Safe, independently testable additions from this review are VBL and Engadin Bus ordinary-bicycle rules, national-product acceptance for the specifically verified operators, Thurbo no-reservation/luggage guidance, AAGS line bans, explicit Zug lake product exceptions, and source-linked local fare formulas. Exact dated service bans retain priority; ordinary rules do not establish special-bicycle fit.

Remaining implementation work:

- Bind provider operator IDs to reviewed companies; do not match unrelated lines solely by a number such as 601.
- Build a route-aware zone/tax-point resolver, including skipped stops, traversed zones, double-counted city zones, overlap master tariffs and fare time limits.
- Resolve conflicting Zug line-601 loading-stop lists and PostAuto special-bike/child wording. Contact operators only for facts not settled by their current authoritative tariff.
- Add profile products that public rules support (e.g. RhB monthly bicycle pass) with operator coverage and effective dates.
- Audit all additional operators/line exceptions beyond this ledger; this is broad German-speaking coverage, not a completed census of every Swiss transport company.
- Validate output against equivalent official purchase searches and representative real journeys. Keep current/retrieved standard fares distinguishable from live production quotations.
