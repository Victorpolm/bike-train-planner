## Objective and current scope

**Decision:** Build a Switzerland-first bicycle + public-transport journey planner. The default keeps the bicycle throughout transit. Beginning/end-only placement can instead leave the bicycle at the first boarding station or use one already at the last alighting station; the opposite endpoint uses a checked pedestrian path. Parking remains an information layer: a particular rack, its availability, parking time/cost and rack-to-platform path are not selected or verified.

**User's requested priority order, 29 September:** (1) bike parking, (2) bike shops/repair referencing, (3) the broader user-interface improvement. The broader proposed scope remains in [APP_ROADMAP.md](APP_ROADMAP.md) and [BIKE_PARKING.md](BIKE_PARKING.md).

## Later decisions and uncertainties

- Passenger-only transit and walking endpoints are implemented using the existing placement control. Choosing a specific rack, parking-entry/exit conditions, cost, parking time and a return/retrieval plan remain open; the current choice assumes the bicycle can be left at or is already available at the selected station.
- Baseline 0 / Extended up to 2 and cycling position are implemented. Next measure live route quality, runtime and truncation; walking now connects addresses on the non-cycling side; through fares across cycling gaps and more than two automatic connections remain open. [Scope and checks](MULTIPLE_CYCLING_TRANSFERS.md).
- Station entrances/pathways, timetable completeness, riding calibration, real user value and the four-versus-eight station-pair experiment remain open.
- Later road-safety work should examine junction manoeuvres, separation from motor traffic and speed/access evidence; a low speed limit, a traffic signal or the absence of recorded crashes cannot establish a universal safety rating.
- Engine evaluation, production national timetable hosting and richer commuting/bikepacking/expert modes remain later work. New hosting spend still needs the owner's approval.
- Native apps, ticket/parking sales, reservation/space booking, social/community features and international expansion are deferred.
