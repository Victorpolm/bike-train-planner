SwitzerlandMobility / FEDRO / cantons — cycling routes and closures via geo.admin.ch

licence
Open Government Data, open use with source attribution (BY); GeoIV Article 21. Applies to the Veloland geometry and closure data. Texts/photos/logos have separate conditions.
attribution
Bundesamt für Strassen, Kanton, Stiftung SchweizMobil; official route name/number and link retained.
caching
App: route lookups 24 hours, closure lookups 15 minutes in bounded server memory. No browser bulk download. Source geometries update periodically; request time is not an edition or field-observation date.
redistribution
Open-use geometry under attribution. Re.route alterations/connectors are explicitly identified. No marketing descriptions, photographs, logos, partner iframe or Komoot tours copied.

status
Enabled for attributed geometry; service availability and physical conditions not guaranteed.
