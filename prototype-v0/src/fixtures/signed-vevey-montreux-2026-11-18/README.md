# Vevey–Montreux signed cycling comparison

Recorded from [BRouter](https://brouter.de/brouter/) on 18 November 2026 between (46.46315, 6.84458) and (46.43534, 6.90987). The fixture holds the two complete GeoJSON responses, gzip-compressed and base64-encoded. Parameters came from the normal cycling client with Fastest, a 15 km/h unassisted rider, and the trekking and fastbike profiles. The test parses both responses and reruns selection with the checkbox off and on. Real-world traffic counts and temporary closures remain unverified.
